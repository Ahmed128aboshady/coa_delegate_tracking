Workflow

How It Works - Three Simple Steps

From delegate smartphone to manager dashboard in seconds. No training, no configuration, no hassle.

1

Delegate Opens Odoo in Mobile Browser

The field delegate opens their company's Odoo URL in any smartphone browser - Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone. They log in with their existing Odoo credentials. No additional app installation is needed whatsoever.

2

Shares GPS Location with One Click

The delegate navigates to the Location Sharing screen within Odoo's Sales menu and taps "Share My Location." The browser's native GPS API captures coordinates and transmits them to Odoo's backend securely - the delegate can then optionally log a customer visit against a partner record.

3

Manager Sees Live Pin on Map

The sales manager's dashboard updates immediately - a pin appears on the OpenStreetMap canvas at the delegate's exact position. The manager can hover or click the pin to view the delegate's name, avatar, last-seen timestamp, and most recent customer visit - all without leaving the Odoo interface.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from sales managers and IT teams.

Q

Do delegates need a special app to share their location?

No - absolutely zero app installation is required. Delegates simply open their company's Odoo web address in any modern smartphone browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Samsung Internet, etc.) and log in with their standard Odoo credentials. The location sharing feature is fully embedded in the Odoo web application and uses the browser's native Geolocation API. There is nothing to download from the App Store or Google Play, no MDM enrollment, and no IT provisioning needed for delegate devices.

Q

How accurate is the GPS location data?

Accuracy depends on the delegate's device and environment, typically ranging from 3 to 20 meters. The module uses the browser's W3C Geolocation API, which on modern smartphones leverages a combination of GPS satellite signals, Wi-Fi triangulation, and cell tower data. Outdoors with clear sky visibility, accuracy is typically within 5 meters. In dense urban areas or indoors, Wi-Fi and cell tower fallback bring accuracy to 1550 meters. The module always stores the accuracy radius alongside the coordinates so managers can assess confidence. This level of accuracy is more than sufficient for field sales management use cases.

Q

Can I see visit history for a specific delegate or customer?

Yes - full visit history is available with powerful filtering options. The Visit Log section (accessible to both managers and delegates, with managers seeing all records) provides a complete timestamped history of every customer visit. You can filter by delegate, by customer (partner), by date range, or by geographical area. The view supports standard Odoo list, grouped list, and pivot table views, enabling managers to produce daily, weekly, or monthly visit frequency reports per delegate or per customer account. All records are also exportable to Excel (XLSX) directly from the list view toolbar.
